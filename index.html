5) If you don’t see a download button, that model may be view-only on Sketchfab.

About us

We, the Cardiac Crew, are a group of USYD undergrad students undertaking the Anatomy and Histology Interdisciplinary Project unit of study (ANAT3888) during semester 2 2025.

"Heartscapes" is our exhibit. We specifically designed it to present the internal anatomical features of the heart from different perspectives.

We really hope you enjoy (and hopefully learn something) from our exhibit!

Augmented Reality (AR)
If on, we’ll open the camera view.

Quick guide

AR on: allow camera → point at a marker → panel appears → tap “Open page”.
AR off: browse the exhibit pages normally.

How to use

To turn AR mode ON/OFF: tap the ⚙️ icon.

AR mode: Use your phone’s camera to recognise the exhibit object, then tap “Open page” to learn more. You will need to allow camera permissions.
Web mode: Browse exhibit pages from the Exhibits list without using the camera (turn AR Off).

Tips

• Hold your phone steady and keep the marker well lit.
• If the marker leaves view, details stay briefly so you can still open the page.
• Use 🔊 in the panel to play audio for each object.
