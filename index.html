⬇ 3D files

If you’d like to download the 3D files designed by us (free) from Sketchfab, here’s the link:

Heartscapes 3D model collection (Sketchfab)

How to download

1) Open the Sketchfab link above.
2) Tap a model you want.
3) Look for Download 3D Model (availability depends on the model’s settings).
4) Choose the format offered (e.g., .STL / .OBJ / .GLTF) and save to your device.
5) If you don’t see a download button, that model may be view-only on Sketchfab.
