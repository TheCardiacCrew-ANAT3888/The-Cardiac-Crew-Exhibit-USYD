Trabeculae Carneae

Interactive Embroidery & Textbook Comparison

In the internal space of the heart's bottom chamber, known as ventricles, there are a series of muscular irregular structures known as the trabeculae carnea. Trabeculae carnea means meaty ridges in Latin. The trabeculae carnea have a unique beauty in their design and provide structural support to the heart while also assisting in pumping blood from the bottom chamber to the top chamber and also around the body.

These patchwork embroidery sections display the internal features of the left ventricle of the heart. The combination of patchwork and embroidery demonstrates the textural aspects of the trabeculae carnea.
